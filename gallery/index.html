There is a little room beside the reading, between one thought and the next. A stem finds its way into it. A leaf unfolds.

Églantine grows these gardens from a seed, then draws them in the manner of an old engraving. No two seeds give quite the same arrangement.

As the day passes, buds open and flowers go to seed. A fuller day brings more leaves. A quiet day still has its flowers.
